# NafPOS — brand guide

NafPOS (from "Naf's POS") is the platform brand for the motorcycle spare parts SaaS.
Individual shops keep their own identity; NafPOS is what the platform, the marketing
site and the "Powered by" receipt footer use.

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Background | Charcoal | `#111214` |
| Primary / accent | Orange | `#FF5B14` |
| Text & light surfaces | Off-white | `#F5F5F2` |

Files: `nafpos-logo.png` (full NafPOS wordmark lockup, charcoal background), `naf-icon.png` (app icon, gear + N mark), `naf-logo.png` (legacy pre-rebrand artwork, kept for reference), `brand.css` (colour + font tokens).

## Positioning
- One product, many Kenyan parts shops: **duka la sehemu** energy — hands, grease, boda boda stages, mechanic slang.
- Speak like a shopkeeper, never like enterprise software: "Sell", "Deni", "Fundi", "Risiti", "Kibandari".
- English and Kiswahili side by side everywhere the user works (POS, till, menus, receipts).
- Promise: sell fast, even offline; get paid by M-Pesa; never lose track of a single shilling.

## Rules for the app
- **Dark theme first** (charcoal background, off-white text) — easy on the eyes and battery-friendly on cheap Android phones.
- **Buttons:** orange background with **charcoal text** (not white). White on this orange is only 2.8:1 contrast (hard to read in sunlight); charcoal on orange is 6.0:1.
- **Orange text on charcoal** is fine (6.0:1) for headings, links and badges.
- **Headings:** bold condensed capitals (logo style) — Barlow Condensed from Google Fonts is a close match. Body text: Inter.
- Each shop (tenant) can upload its own logo (`tenants.logo_path`); this brand is used for the platform: landing page, login/signup, and the receipt footer "Powered by NafPOS".
- Wordmark: always **NafPOS** (capital N, capital P, capital S). Never "Nafpos" / "NAF POS".
